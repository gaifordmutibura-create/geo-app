About the Project

This GIS dashboard provides an interactive platform for mapping and analysing illegal dumpsites within Highfields, Harare, Zimbabwe. The system allows users to visualise the study boundary, individual dumpsite classes and associated spatial information.

The dashboard forms part of a GIS-based approach to spatial analysis and environmental risk assessment of illegal dumpsites.
